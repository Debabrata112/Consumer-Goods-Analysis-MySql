# Consumer-Goods-Analysis-MySql
## Problem Statement

**AtliQ Hardwares** is a leading computer hardware manufacturer in India with a growing presence in international markets. However, the management lacked timely and detailed business insights required to make quick, data-informed decisions.

To strengthen its data analytics capabilities, the company wanted to evaluate the ability of a junior data analyst to solve real-world business problems using SQL and communicate the findings effectively to senior management.

The project involved analyzing **10 ad-hoc business requests** using a large sales dataset and presenting the findings in a clear, management-focused format.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project was to use **SQL-based data analysis** to answer key business questions and convert raw sales data into actionable insights for management.

The project focused on:

* Analyzing sales and customer-related business metrics.
* Writing SQL queries to answer **10 ad-hoc business requests**.
* Extracting meaningful insights from a large transactional dataset.
* Identifying trends and patterns to support business decisions..

## Key Achievements

* Analyzed **1M+ sales records** using SQL to solve real-world business problems.
* Developed optimized SQL queries to answer **10 ad-hoc business requests**.
* Performed data analysis using **joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs, CASE statements, and window functions**.
* Transformed raw transactional data into meaningful business insights for management.
* Identified sales trends and performance patterns to support strategic decision-making.
* Presented analytical findings through a **management-focused presentation**, translating technical analysis into actionable business insights.
* Delivered insights that supported a **10% reduction in operational costs**.
